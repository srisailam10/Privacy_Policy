# Privacy Policy for GoatKart

**Last updated:** October 07, 2026

**GoatKart** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by GoatKart.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided Directly by You
We may collect personal details that you voluntarily provide to us when using GoatKart, such as:
* **Account Details:** Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address during account creation or checkout.
* **Order Details:** Purchase history, cart contents, and customer support inquiries.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
When you access or use the app, we may automatically collect certain device data, including:
* Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
* Log files, IP address, usage data, and crash reports.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
* Provide, operate, and maintain GoatKart features and services.
* Process transactions and manage user orders.
* Improve performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.
* Send transaction updates, order confirmations, and customer support notifications.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We **do not sell** your personal information. We only share data with trusted third-party service providers necessary for app operations, such as:
* **Google Play Services:** For app installation, core Android services, and analytics.
* **Payment Processors:** To securely process payments (financial data is handled according to PCI-DSS standards).

---

## 4. Data Retention & Security

We retain collected information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

---
